Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 05, 2026

This Privacy Notice applies to all software packages, binaries, and websites provided by TurboReplace Technologies ("FastAlgos", "we", "us", or "our").

1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Our software binaries execute 100% autonomously and locally on your target hardware. We collect strictly zero telemetry, analytics, or behavioral data. The only information processed is the payment infrastructure handled by our Merchant of Record, Paddle, when you purchase a license (e.g., email address, name, and billing details).

2. HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?

We do not process any personal data from your local system execution. The transactional data collected during purchase is processed solely to fulfill your license order, generate custom cryptographic watermarks for your binary, and verify software authenticity.

3. WHAT LEGAL BASES DO WE RELY ON TO PROCESS YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION?

We process your personal information only when necessary to perform our contractual obligations (delivering the software license) and to comply with legal tax reporting mandates governing international software sales.

4. WHEN AND WITH WHOM DO WE SHARE YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION?

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. Your transactional information is shared exclusively with Paddle, our payment gateway provider, to process transactions securely and prevent fraudulent activities.

5. WHAT IS OUR STANCE ON THIRD-PARTY WEBSITES?

Our website may contain links to external checkout networks (Paddle) or documentation repositories (GitHub). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party domains. Please review their policies upon redirection.

6. DO WE USE COOKIES AND OTHER TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES?

Our infrastructure utilizes zero tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or hidden tracking pixels. We use standard browser storage mechanisms (like Service Workers cache) strictly to optimize offline frontend GUI performance.

7. HOW LONG DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION?

We retain transaction-related data (such as license keys and purchase email logs) for as long as your lifetime license remains active, or as required by applicable tax laws.

8. HOW DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION SAFE?

All database communications inside our backend network are locked down natively. Transaction processing is fully delegated to Paddle’s bank-grade secure environment. We do not store raw credit card details on our local servers.

9. DO WE COLLECT INFORMATION FROM MINORS?

We do not knowingly solicit or collect data from individuals under 18 years of age. Our academic licenses require valid institutional confirmation.

10. WHAT ARE YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS?

Depending on your jurisdiction (such as the GDPR for European users), you have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of the email address associated with your license file. Contact us directly to exercise these rights.

11. CONTROLS FOR DO-NOT-TRACK FEATURES

Since we do not track browser history or collect behavioral telemetry, our software platforms are natively fully compliant with all Do-Not-Track (DNT) browser headers.

12. DO CALIFORNIA RESIDENTS HAVE SPECIFIC PRIVACY RIGHTS?

Yes. Under the CCPA/CPRA, California residents have the right to know what personal data is collected and request its erasure. As noted, we do not sell personal data or track your digital activity.

13. DO VIRGINIA RESIDENTS HAVE SPECIFIC PRIVACY RIGHTS?

Yes. Under the VCDPA, Virginia consumers have identical protections regarding data access and deletion. We conform to these requirements by enforcing zero-tracking standards.

14. DO WE MAKE UPDATES TO THIS NOTICE?

We may update this privacy notice from time to time to maintain compliance with changing laws. The updated version will be indicated by an updated revision date at the top of this page.

16. HOW CAN YOU REVIEW, UPDATE, OR DELETE THE DATA WE COLLECT FROM YOU?
